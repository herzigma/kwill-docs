## Creating a new users

To create a new user, go to [https://app.kwill.tech/](https://app.kwill.tech) and click Register:

1. Don't forget your password! I haven't implemented password recovery yet.
1. You'll need an access code. Contact me to get one.
1. Check your email for a verification code. Warning: don't close the verification window, or you'll need to contact me to manually verify your account.
